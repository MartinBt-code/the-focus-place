Why it matters

Most game situations — a teammate's pass, an opponent approaching from the side, the ball at the edge of your vision — happen outside your direct line of sight. Good peripheral vision means faster decisions without having to turn your head.

What it measures

The width and speed of your field of vision — how quickly and accurately you notice a stimulus outside the point you're directly looking at.

Who it helps

Team sport athletes (basketball, football, hockey), combat sport athletes, and drivers.

Rating scale

≥ 90%Exceptional peripheral vision
75–89%Good peripheral vision
60–74%Average peripheral vision
< 60%Below average peripheral vision

Wide, fast peripheral vision is typical of experienced team sport players — it helps you sense teammates and opponents outside your direct line of sight without turning your head.
