Why it matters

Holding focus on a repetitive, outwardly boring task is one of the hardest mental skills in sport — the same thing happens during a long tennis match or a full round of golf, when attention has to stay sharp even in moments with no action. A bigger grid means holding concentration for longer without losing pace.

What it measures

The ability to sustain focused attention on a repetitive visual task over a longer stretch of time, without it "switching off" or getting distracted.

Who it helps

Athletes in disciplines with long duration and high concentration demands (tennis, golf, shooting), and anyone who wants to train their attention endurance.
