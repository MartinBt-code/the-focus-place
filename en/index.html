Why it matters

Reaction time is the foundation of almost every sport — goals, defense, and getting past an opponent are decided in fractions of a second. Regular reaction training shortens the gap between perception and movement and reduces the number of missed moments.

What it measures

How fast you react to a simple visual stimulus — the time between the color change and your click, measured in milliseconds.

Who it helps

Athletes across all disciplines, drivers, and anyone who wants to track their psychomotor performance over time.
